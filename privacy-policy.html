Job Application Autofill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Summary

Job Application Autofill does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the extension stores

The profile you type in (for example your name, contact details, address, work and education history, answers to common application questions) and any resume or cover letter file you add are saved using the browser's local extension storage on your computer. They are never uploaded to any server. The developer has no server and cannot see your data.

How the data is used

Only when you click "Fill this page" or press the keyboard shortcut, the extension reads your saved profile and writes it into the form fields on the page you are viewing. It does not read, record or send the contents of the pages you visit. Submitting a form is always your own action, and what you submit goes only to that website, under that website's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: keep your profile and resume file on your device.
  • activeTab / scripting: fill the form on the current tab, only when you click Fill.
  • Access to all websites (optional, off by default): lets the extension fill application forms embedded in frames from job-application providers. You can turn it on or off in the extension's settings.

Analytics and third parties

The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code, and makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

Clear the fields on the profile page, or remove the extension; the browser then deletes all of its stored data.
